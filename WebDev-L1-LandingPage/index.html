The Process

Good coffee takes time

Sourced

Directtrade beans

We partner directly with Indian coffee farms, choosing each lot after careful tasting and ensuring farmers are paid fairly.

Roasted

Profiled, not templated

Every coffee gets its own roast profile, carefully refined batch after batch to bring out the unique character of the bean not just a standard dark roast flavour.

Delivered

Bagged within 48 hours

Beans rest just long enough to de-gas, then ship straight to your door,usually less than two days after they came out of the drum.

About Savera

Started in a garage. Still roasts like it.

Savera Coffee Roasters began in 2025 with a single second-hand roaster and a stubborn belief that most "specialty" coffee wasn't actually that special. one years later, we've grown to a small storefront and a three-person roasting team — but the batches are still tiny on purpose, and every bag still gets tasted before it ships.

We're not trying to be everywhere. We're trying to be worth seeking out.
